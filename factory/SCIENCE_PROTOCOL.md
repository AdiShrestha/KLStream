# Scientific protocol — local Factory 3.3.1

Register every claim's estimand, population, unit of analysis, split, metric,
direction, selection/stopping policy and evidence path before confirmation.
Preserve raw observations, IDs, lineage, failed attempts and prior exposure.
Recompute from raw rows through a distinct implementation; agreement validates
that calculation, not the population's authenticity or every shared assumption.

Replication counts and measurement budgets need prospective precision or power
reasoning at the actual independent unit. Five seeds, ten epochs, arbitrary
percentage perturbations and full-factorial coverage are defaults or design tools,
not universal scientific requirements. Seeds on one corpus do not create new
datasets. Fixed-budget nonconvergence, flat sensitivity, zero observed failures
and negative effects remain evidence with appropriate limits. Do not extend a
budget selectively after seeing unfavorable results. A convergence claim requires
method-specific support; Isolation Forest has no gradient-loss epochs.

Use credible tuned alternatives, matched resource opportunities and operationally
isolated ablations. Declare interaction identifiability and aliasing. Select
sensitivity ranges from plausible mechanism/workload regimes on development data.
Keep holdout labels out of fitting and selection; a local freeze is not a sealed
holdout. For temporal systems, preserve causal ordering and observed class balance
rather than imposing artificial stratification that changes the target population.

Simulation may support explicitly scoped simulation claims when the model and
assumptions are justified. Fixtures support correctness checks only. Observational
claims require authentic sources; generators cannot stand in for missing market
data or labels. Unlabelled systems experiments must not invent labels merely to
fit this factory's current binary-classification profile.

Hardware claims require actual native execution, raw trial endpoints, declared
warmup/exclusion rules, process/device scopes and measured or unavailable resources.
Service time and completion rate do not establish offered-load end-to-end behavior.
The streaming/native profile is still unimplemented; root KLS-02/KLS-07 must be
accepted before KLStream research execution. No active research epoch exists.

Certification is a bounded assessment of current evidence and recorded review.
It cannot guarantee scientific truth, novelty, universal stability or acceptance.
