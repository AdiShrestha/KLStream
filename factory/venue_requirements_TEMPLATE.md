# Venue requirements template

Record the exact venue/version/date and primary URL. Summarize applicable reporting, data/code/protocol, statistical, compute, supplementary, preregistration, and disclosure requirements into `project/methodology.md`. For each requirement give a manuscript location, artifact path, hash, and status. “The venue requires rigor” is not evidence.

### Evidence sufficiency

State prospective precision/sample-size reasoning, appropriate split/imbalance handling and method-specific fitting evidence for each comparative claim. Budget-limited nonconvergence and natural imbalance are admissible with scoped interpretation. Historical Factory defaults do not establish sufficiency.
